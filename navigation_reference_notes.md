# Original Navigation Reference Notes

## Base reference, crops 1–2

- The header uses a Warm Cream ground with a single thin neutral divider below it.
- The lockup is left-aligned within the content container: a compact red-and-black mark followed by a two-line uppercase **GROTHWORKS / SYSTEMS** wordmark.
- The primary desktop navigation begins after a generous gap from the lockup and reads, in order: **Revenue Infrastructure**, **Solutions**, and **Industries**. Each of these three grouped labels carries a small downward chevron.

## Base reference, crops 3–4

The navigation continues with plain-text **Resources** and **About** links. A solid Growth Crimson rectangular **Book Discovery Call** button anchors the far right. The reference has no utility control between the CTA and the primary navigation.

## Industries menu, crops 1–2

The active grouped link changes to Growth Crimson and gains a fine Crimson underline. Its white dropdown begins directly below the header divider, aligns to the grouped trigger, and uses a subtle neutral border and soft elevation. The menu presents the first two visible industry entries as **Home Services** with “Built for home service businesses” and **Financial Advisors & RIAs** with “Retirement planners & wealth advisors.”

The third industry entry is **Insurance Agencies** with “Independent agencies & brokerages.” The active trigger chevron rotates upward while its menu is open. The menu is intentionally compact and stays clear of the right-aligned CTA.

## Revenue Infrastructure menu, crops 1–2

When **Revenue Infrastructure** is active, it uses the same Crimson text, fine underline, and upward chevron treatment. Its dropdown follows the same white, lightly elevated panel pattern and contains **The Framework** with “Nine domains. One operating system.” followed by **Diagnostic Platform** with “Methodology, scorecard, and roadmap.”

The Revenue Infrastructure panel is narrower than the Industries panel because it contains two entries. Resources and About remain in their baseline positions while the dropdown overlays the hero beneath the navigation divider.

## Solutions menu, crops 1–2

The **Solutions** trigger uses the same active Crimson underline and upward chevron treatment. Its wider white dropdown lists four concise solution entries: **AI Visibility** (“Be found by AI-powered search”), **AI-Ready Website** (“Convert attention into trust”), **CRM & Automation** (“Eliminate revenue leakage”), and **Conversion Systems** (“Turn interest into pipeline”).

The Solutions panel is the widest of the three dropdowns but remains aligned beneath its trigger and does not obscure the far-right CTA. Each entry is a simple title-and-description pair with no decorative card treatment.

## Footer reference, crops 1–2

The footer uses an almost-black ground split from the page by a fine neutral top rule and a separate low-contrast bottom bar. The first column carries the official logo in white, followed by “Build. Automate. Grow.”, a concise Revenue Infrastructure descriptor, and mail, telephone, and LinkedIn contact rows with muted Crimson icons. The next two columns use small uppercase Growth Crimson headings for **Revenue Infrastructure** and **Solutions** and muted-light link lists.

The final two footer columns are **Industries** and **Company**. Industries includes Home Services, Financial Advisors & RIAs, and Insurance Agencies. Company includes About GWS, Resources, Contact, and Book Discovery Call. The bottom bar pairs a left-aligned copyright with the italic line “One System. Every Touchpoint. Predictable Revenue.” on the right.
