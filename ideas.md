# GrowthWorks Systems Homepage — Governing Design Direction

## Ground-Truth Specification

This implementation follows the user-supplied GrowthWorks Systems homepage materials as the authoritative design direction. It is a **faithful production build**, not a new design exploration. Source precedence is: latest governing/ruling specifications; approved desktop/mobile wireframes; section-specific copy; typography, spacing, color and CTA documentation; approved visual/diagram specifications; supporting rationale; legacy/current-site material and inventory.

## Chosen Approach: Editorial Revenue Infrastructure

**Design Movement:** Restrained editorial systems design, with the clarity and authority of a considered business publication rather than a generic SaaS landing page.

**Core Principles:** One focal point per section; generous whitespace and clear downward hierarchy; precise typographic contrast; systems relationships made understandable without relying on motion or hover.

**Color Philosophy:** Use only the documented GWS color system and CTA states. Color is functional: it creates a calm credible field, distinguishes the Revenue Infrastructure relationship, and makes action states unmistakable without ornamental treatments.

**Layout Paradigm:** An 11-section narrative progression from recognition of fragmented revenue performance, through a connected system, to outcomes, application areas, credibility, and a diagnostic CTA. The page uses editorial bands, diagram-led moments, and deliberate changes in density rather than interchangeable card grids.

**Signature Elements:** The static Revenue Infrastructure centerpiece; restrained rule-lines and typographic labels; a compact four-step methodology anchor: Diagnose → Design → Implement → Optimize.

**Interaction Philosophy:** Essential information is permanently comprehensible. Accordions, where approved, use accessible one-open-at-a-time disclosure only for secondary detail. Interaction never substitutes for clarity.

**Animation:** No decorative motion. Any transition is restrained, limited to opacity and transform, respects reduced-motion preferences, and may only reinforce an already visible relationship.

**Typography System:** DM Serif Display is reserved for display hierarchy and section headings. DM Sans serves navigation, body copy, labels, controls, captions, and utility text. Calibri is excluded from production UI.

**Brand Essence:** GrowthWorks Systems helps businesses replace fragmented revenue activity with a connected system for being found, responding, converting, and improving. **Personality:** credible, deliberate, systems-oriented.

**Brand Voice:** Direct, precise, confident, and grounded in operational clarity—not inflated marketing language. Example lines: “Your revenue performance should work as one connected system.” and “See where revenue is leaking before you invest in another tool.”

**Wordmark & Logo:** Preserve or derive only from approved source materials. Until a documented asset is confirmed, use a simple typographic GrowthWorks Systems wordmark treatment rather than inventing a replacement brand mark.

**Signature Brand Color:** Use the documented GWS primary action color exactly as specified in the governing color and CTA document.

## Build Check

Before accepting a visual or code decision, ask: **Does this reinforce the approved editorial, restrained, credible, premium, systems-oriented direction—or dilute it?**

## Approved Visual Reference Findings

The supplied AI Buyer Journey visual is a dark, self-contained applied-example composition. It contrasts **Traditional Search** with **AI-Assisted Discovery**, visually emphasizing a shortlist formed before a website visit. It should sit as the single explanatory asset in Section 07, beside the concise approved copy, rather than being duplicated with a new AI diagram.

The supplied desktop Revenue Infrastructure reference is a light editorial three-stage composition: a left **Disconnected Tools** diagnostic column, a central **Revenue Infrastructure** resolution point with four connected capabilities and the compact methodology anchor, and a right **Business Outcomes** column. The implementation must preserve this problem → connected system → outcome reading direction while using the newer mobile specification to update terminology and narrow-layout interaction behavior.

## Style Decisions

- Major sections use distinct editorial formats, including a diagnostic ledger, an outcome rule-line framework, a numbered methodology strip, and a coordinated application-area system. The required four-card and 2×2 module structures remain, but are rendered as connected structural fields rather than generic isolated SaaS cards.
- The Revenue Infrastructure signature relationship—fragmented activity → one connected operating system → measurable outcomes—recurs through numbering, directional rule-lines, concise labels, and structured progression throughout the page.
- GrowthWorks Systems remains a restrained typographic wordmark. Its scale, serif contrast, and disciplined Growth Crimson emphasis are consistently reinforced in header, founder, and footer placements.
